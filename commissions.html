About Commissions:

Pricing schema is based on the complexity of the project. My methodology is simple: more work = more money, less work = less money. No base rates, no flat fees, just paying for the work that is done. I will provide a quote for the project after discussing the details of the commission with you. However I can provide a rough estimate of the cost based on prior experience:

Price Estimates:

(All prices are in USD)
  • Custom texture: $40-$60
    • Price ranges based on complexity of texture. Note that prices can be even less, especially if it is simply a recolour of the existing texture.
  • Mesh edits: $80-$100
    • Mesh edits are difficult. Depending on what part is modified, this can result in having to modify clothing or other accessories to match. If the model was improperly exported by the original creator, this can result in issues when reimporting, resulting in me having to reassemble the entire model in Unity (fixing Physbones, scripts, etc.).
  • Custom accessories: $10-$40
    • Pricing for this can vary greatly depending on complexity. Whether it is just a static prop or whether it has functions changes the price greatly.

VRChat

  • Avatars:
    • Modification to working functions of an existing avatar
    • NEW! Kitbashing of avatars (combining parts of existing avatars, for example, swapping the head on an avatar with a different head)
