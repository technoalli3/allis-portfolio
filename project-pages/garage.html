Description

I love working on cars. I love cars in general; they fascinate me and intrigue me with all of their complicated workings. With that in lieu, I created a VRChat world that serves as a safe space for myself. Modeled and textured entirely from scratch, this cozy garage features my signature Bel Air, as well as a van chassis that has been repurposed as a blanket fort. This is one of my latest worlds and features my latest work.
